Privacy Policy

Within a Deep Forest (Android), package com.deepforest.game.
Last updated: 17 October 2026 (Play Games added).

The game keeps your progress only on your device. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or in-app purchases of its own.

Google Play Games

Leaderboards and achievements use Google Play Games. If you are signed in to Play Games, the game sends Google the balls you find, whether you reached the ending, and the play time it took (as leaderboard scores and achievements). They appear with your Play Games profile, as set in your Play Games settings; Google handles this data under the Google Privacy Policy. You can hide your activity or delete your Play Games data in the Play Games settings at any time. Nothing else is sent anywhere.
